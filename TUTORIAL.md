# How to Generate AI Videos for Telegram: Complete Tutorial

This tutorial walks you through creating AI-generated videos optimized for Telegram channels, groups, and communities.

## Why Use a Telegram AI Video Generator?

Telegram has over 900 million monthly active users. Video content drives 3x more engagement than text posts on Telegram channels. A Telegram AI video generator lets you create professional videos in minutes without editing software.

## Step 1: Prepare Your Content Idea

Before generating, decide:
- Topic: What message does your video convey?
- Format: Square (1:1) for feeds, vertical (9:16) for stories
- Length: 15-60 seconds works best for Telegram

## Step 2: Write a Good Prompt

Effective prompts include:
- Subject and action
- Visual style (cinematic, anime, realistic)
- Camera movement (pan, zoom, static)
- Lighting and mood

Example prompt: "A futuristic city at sunset, flying cars, cinematic wide shot, warm orange lighting, slow pan left"

1. Open your Telegram channel or group

5. Post

## Best Practices for Telegram Videos

- Keep it short: Under 60 seconds for maximum retention
- Add captions: Many users watch without sound
- Use hooks: Start with the most engaging frame
- Post consistently: 3-5 videos per week grows channels fastest
